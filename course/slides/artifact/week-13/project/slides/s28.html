deploy.yml is public, with your pool id and deployer email in it. Someone forks it.

What stops them deploying into your project?

Week 13 · Infrastructure as code, environments, pipelines

28

Let them squirm briefly. The attribute condition: trust is bound to your-org/your-repo, and a fork's token asserts a different repository, so the exchange fails. The pool id is configuration — it grants nothing on its own. A key would have been the opposite. Generalise: OIDC federation is how every CI system should now authenticate to every cloud.
