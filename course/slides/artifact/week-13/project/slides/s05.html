The script fails, or worse, makes a second thing. Terraform does nothing: the world already matches. That is idempotency — week 11's word, applied to infrastructure. "Set the balance to 400", not "add 100". Then the honest cost: you must describe everything; the tool's ordering opinions are sometimes wrong; and you now have a state file to look after.
