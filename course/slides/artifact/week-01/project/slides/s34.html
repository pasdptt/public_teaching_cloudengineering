The four

  1. A storage bucket with confidential files was readable by anyone on the internet.
  1. A zone lost power; every service in it stopped for four hours.
  1. A guest OS vulnerability on a VM was exploited three weeks after the patch was published.
  1. A managed database was unreachable for 20 minutes during a maintenance window announced a month in advance, and the customer’s application had no retry logic.
